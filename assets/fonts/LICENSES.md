# Bundled typefaces

Both are self-hosted (no external requests) as the Google Fonts `latin` subset,
covering U+0000–00FF — enough for every band and venue name in the data, plus
room for future ones.

Both licenses permit redistribution, and both require the license to travel with
the font — which is why the full texts sit in this directory rather than being
linked.

| `permanent-marker-latin.woff2` | Permanent Marker, single weight | Apache License 2.0 — [`LICENSE-PermanentMarker.txt`](LICENSE-PermanentMarker.txt) |
